Autoselect Secure Warranty Program

The strength and attraction of the product is the three levels of cover which offers dealers and their customers a comprehensive choice of first class warranty cover depending on their needs.

Secure: A comprehensive listed component cover.

Platinum: All mechanical and electrical cover derived from a manufacturer used car warranty program. Also cover for vehicles of any age and mileage.

Platinum Plus: Wear and Tear cover on all covered components. Additional components covered included Satellite Navigation System, Multimedia, Diesel Particulate Filter, Catalytic Converter and even the battery.

Warranty FAQs

What our 6-month cover includes, how to extend it, and how claims work.

Every car priced over £5,000 comes with a minimum 6-month warranty, provided through Autoselect and supported by the AA. It covers the major mechanical and electrical components against unforeseen failure, so you have real peace of mind after purchase.

Yes. We offer extended platinum warranty packages through Autoselect for vehicles and cover levels beyond the standard 6 months. Longer cover suits higher-mileage cars or drivers who want to keep their car for the long haul. Ask us for a quote on a specific vehicle.

As with most used-car warranties, normal wear and tear, routine servicing, tyres and consumables are not covered. The warranty is there to protect you against unexpected mechanical or electrical failure, not everyday running costs.

You can raise a claim directly with Autoselect, our warranty provider, using the paperwork supplied with your car. We are happy to help you understand the process, so get in touch if you need assistance.

The remaining cover transfers with the vehicle to the next owner, as long as the terms of the policy are maintained. It is a further reason our cars hold their value well.

Cookie Preferences

Cookies are small data files that websites store on your device (computer, phone, tablet) when you visit them. They act like tiny pieces of information websites remember about you. Here are some key things to know about cookies:

  • They make websites work better: Cookies can help websites remember things you've done on previous visits.
  • They personalise your experience: Cookies can be used to show you content relevant to your interests and browsing habits.
  • They provide website analytics: Websites can use cookies to understand how visitors use their site, helping them improve your experience.
It's important to note:

Cookies typically don't store personal information that identifies you directly (like your name or email address).
